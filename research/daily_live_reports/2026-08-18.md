## Operations And Bugs

1. **The nervous-system Postgres is dead and nothing noticed.**
   `nervous-system-execution-postgres-1` exited 255 at 2026-08-18T04:21:57Z
   (00:21:57 ET), one minute before the supervisor came up — it went down with
   the WSL restart and was never brought back. Yesterday's container log shows
   repeated `canceling statement due to lock timeout` while locking the
   `nervous_system.alerts` row for `execution.gateway|AMD`, so the shutdown was
   not clean. Impact: **every Meta ranker order today was lost**, silently, with
   only a stack trace in the server log. Nothing alerted, the watchdog did not
   fire, and the module reported no error to any dashboard. Fix is two parts —
   restart the container, and make the gateway degrade to the direct submit path
   (or at minimum defer to `pending_open_entries.json`) instead of raising out of
   `main()` with orders in hand.
2. **Options buying power exhausted; the sizer does not check it.** Every entry
   is sized to a ~$5,000 target notional, but `options_buying_power` sat between
   $2,378 and $4,700 for the whole session while total buying power was $1.34M.
   13 buys were rejected. The rejections cluster within a few hundred dollars of
   the limit, so a pre-submit check against `options_buying_power` (and shrinking
   the contract count to fit) would have recovered most of them.
3. **Stale entries are accumulating in the pending-open queues.** Meta holds 3
   entries from the 08-17 18:00Z bar (PLUG 2,237 sh, AMBA 64 sh, GTLB 123 sh)
   and HTF holds one from **08-14** (LPTH260821C00015000 x49 — an option that
   expires this Friday) plus one from 08-17. These have survived multiple
   pre-open flushes. Executing a 4-day-old signal at tomorrow's open is a real
   risk; the queue needs a staleness expiry.
4. **The stale-mark guard is firing, but its "live mid" is the unreliable
   number.** 28 `STALE option mark` warnings (APTV x21, MOD x3, VIAV x2, EW,
   ARMK). The guard is working as designed — it re-polls a quote and re-runs the
   exit rule on it. The problem is which side is actually stale. On the two that
   went on to trade, the *broker* mark was right and the re-polled mid was wrong:
   EW flagged "broker 0.2 vs live mid 0.9950" and filled at **0.20**; APTV
   flagged "broker 0.05 vs live mid 0.0800" and filled at **0.05**. These are
   wide one-sided books (TGT's was bid 3.65 / ask 5.92, 47% of mid), where the
   midpoint is not a tradeable price. `_corroborate_option_exit_mark`
   (`core/live_4h_exec.py:365`) has no spread guard — it compares broker mark to
   mid and trusts the mid whenever they diverge by more than
   `STALE_OPTION_MARK_DIVERGENCE`. So 20 of the 21 APTV warnings suppressed a
   stop (`'stop_-50%' -> 'hold'`) on an inflated midpoint. Suggested fix: skip
   the correction when `spread_pct_mid` is wide, and corroborate against the bid
   (what a seller can actually get) rather than the mid.
5. **SPY daytrader opened and closed two positions within seconds.** 18:11:04 ->
   18:11:06 UTC (2s) and 18:52:22 -> 18:53:13 UTC (51s), both `entry_filled`
   then immediately `setup_failure_exit`. Round-trip commission and spread on a
   2-second hold is pure loss. Either the entry confirmation or the exit rule is
   reading a bar the other has not seen.
6. **Swing close-order limit ladder still rarely fills.** TGT walked 4.79 ->
   4.61 -> 4.43 -> 4.25 -> 4.07 across all five attempts, every one `not
   verified via=timeout`, before the market fallback filled at 3.70 — 23% below
   the first rung. Same defect flagged on 08-17.
7. **Meta's exit deferral silently re-claims positions.** At 16:20 the MRVL
   08-21 call exit was deferred and the position "restored to managed state so
   it stays claimed". MRVL expires Friday and its mark fell 62.6% today. If
   tomorrow's flush skips it the way today's skipped 4 of 4, it goes to expiry
   unmanaged.
8. **`Data/inference/intraday_structure/state.json` is now 96.7 MB**, up from
   94 MB yesterday. Growing ~2.5 MB/day with no compaction.
9. **Broker `last_equity` disagrees with our own snapshot by $51,709.** The
   08-17 20:05 ET snapshot recorded $917,178.30; the broker's official prior
   close is $865,469.63. The extended-hours option marks in the 20:05 capture
   overstate. This report uses the broker's `last_equity` baseline, which makes
   the -$88,002 figure conservative relative to a snapshot-to-snapshot delta of

10. Watchdog logged no restarts, no OOM, no missed heartbeats. Server RSS peaked
