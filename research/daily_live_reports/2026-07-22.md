   capture and the 16:45 ET nightly "Dealer snapshots" step failed this way
   — the nightly step explicitly logged `2790/2790 done: summaries=0
   strikes=0 errors=2790` and exited 1. This blocked all Dealer Ranker and
   Amethyst trading today and leaves tomorrow's dealer-level inputs stale.
   Needs a non-interactive token refresh path or a manual re-auth before the
   next scheduled run.
2. **Swing 30m IOT unclosable position.** A restored `IOT260724C00036000`
   call has failed to close on every attempt (77 today) with `HTTP 403:
   account not eligible to trade uncovered option contracts`. This looks
   like an account permission/tier mismatch on a restored position rather
   than a transient error, since it repeated identically across 6.5+ hours;
   worth checking account option-trading level directly rather than
   retrying.
3. **Two-sleeve shadow tracker crash.** `scripts/shadow/two_sleeve_shadow_tracker.py`
   raised `KeyError: 'entry_avg_price'` in `run_module()` at both its 14:35
   and 16:35 ET runs today, while pricing the momentum sleeve's 7th managed
   position (after successfully handling PSNL/FBRX/DMRA/LASR/SILC/FEIM).
   Exit code 1 both times. HTF and Meta shadow trackers never got a turn
   today (their state/audit files are unchanged since 07-21 22:18); only
   momentum's shadow audit picked up a partial update. This is paper-only
   research infrastructure (no order path), so no trading impact, but the
   tracker is not currently producing usable daily output.
4. **Nightly ticker discovery timeout.** The broad screen/catalyst/promotion
   step ran into repeated Yahoo Finance `HTTP 500`/`503` errors starting
   ~18:16 ET and never finished, hitting the wrapper's timeout (exit=124)
   after about an hour. No discovery/promotion stats are available for

   complete status=124`, even though every downstream step (news, themes,
