## Corrections (added 2026-09-04, after the follow-up investigation)

Three claims in this report were wrong or unproven. The evidence is in the
execution database and in direct measurement.

1. **The Meta Ranker outage did not start on 09-02.** This report inferred a
   same-day regression from the 09-01 ledger carrying real broker order IDs.
   Those rows were `stop_-39%`, `underlying_stop_-1.5atr` and `dropped_out`
   exits — all written by the five-minute **risk pass**, which still uses the
   direct broker client and still works. Only the 4H-loop exits (`horizon`,
   `take_profit_+30%`) go through the gateway, and *every* one of those in the
   ledger is a phantom: 25 on 2026-08-27 and 5 on 2026-09-02. The
   `nervous_system.order_requests` table holds 64 rows dating back to
   2026-08-25, all still `PLANNED`, against **zero** rows in
   `submission_attempts` and zero in `execution_events`. Meta's governed path
   has never placed an order.

2. **Root cause, exactly.** `MetaGatewayRouter` set
   `expires_at = decision_bar + DEFAULT_ORDER_TTL` with a TTL of **20 minutes**,
   while the module submits 2h20m to 4h20m after its bar (the 14:20 and 16:20 ET
   loops) and ~19h35m after it at the next session's pre-open flush. Every
   request was therefore refused `PREFLIGHT_REFUSED: the order request has
   expired` before the broker was reached. Postgres was healthy throughout;
   "POSTGRES_UNAVAILABLE is the leading candidate" was wrong, and so was the
   fallback guess of a policy preflight.

3. **The shadow tracker audit gap is not a bug.** `two_sleeve_shadow_tracker`
   saves `<module>_shadow_state.json` unconditionally on every run but appends
   to `<module>_shadow_audit.jsonl` only on a real shadow entry or exit, so
   comparing the two files' mtimes is meaningless. HTF's audit shows 6 entries
   and 4 exits on 09-02; the trail is complete. Item #9 in Operations And Bugs
   should be disregarded.

4. **The SPY loop stall is narrowed, not explained.** The meta feature frame
   build was the obvious suspect and is ruled out: it takes **0.87s** over the
   full 50,000-bar buffer with pivot and TB probabilities enabled. In a window
   where one bar took 1,268s, the other nine cost ~3s between them — so this is
   one phase occasionally hanging for 5-21 minutes, not general slowness, and it
   looks like a blocking call rather than computation. Per-phase timing has been
   added to attribute it on the next occurrence.

Item #7 (Dealer Ranker's NTAP entry "unconfirmed") also resolved on its own:
the ladder left it resting at the ask, `mark_entry_unconfirmed` tracked it, and
it filled at 6.60. That was the mechanism working, not a defect.

## Operations And Bugs

1. **Meta Ranker submitted nothing to the broker all day while reporting
   success (critical, still active).**

   Every Meta order today printed `OK <side> <qty> <symbol>  id=?` and every
   ledger row carries `order_id: "?"` and `realized_pnl: null`. Yesterday's
   rows carry real UUIDs, so this began today.

   Verified against the account, not the logs:

   | Meta claimed | At broker (20:05 ET) |
   |---|---|
   | buy PATH260918C00020000 x55 | not held |
   | buy GTLB260918C00050000 x23 | not held |
   | buy XNCR x179 | not held |
   | buy NVAX260918C00010000 x64 | not held |
   | buy NTSK260918C00015000 x39 | 39 held — HTF's order, id `df7eab3d` |
   | buy DELL260918C00470000 x3 | 3 arrived from HTF's `51560bb0`, less HTF's sale = 2 |
   | sell 100 Z / 100 ZG / 100 KEEL | all three still held at 100 |

   Meta and HTF independently planned the *same two contracts at the same size*
   in their pre-open flushes two minutes apart (DELL x3, NTSK x39). Only HTF's
   arrived. That coincidence is what makes the account arithmetic decisive.

   Mechanism. Meta is the only module routing through
   `DecisionCoordinator` -> `ExecutionGateway`
   (`signals/meta_context/meta_ranker/live_runner.py:1055`). Two defects
   compound:
   - `core/nervous_system/orchestration/coordinator.py:302-309` calls
     `gateway.submit(...)` and then returns `PlanningOutcome(submitted=True)`
     **unconditionally**, without inspecting the returned `ExecutionResult`.
     `ExecutionGateway.submit` (`core/nervous_system/execution/gateway.py:176`)
     can return `outcome=REFUSED` with `broker_order_id=None` and a
     `reason_code` of `PREFLIGHT_REFUSED` or `POSTGRES_UNAVAILABLE`.
   - `_record` in `live_runner.py:1082` gates only on
     `row.refusal is not None or not row.submitted`. A gateway-level refusal
     satisfies neither, so it prints OK, calls `mark_entry_unconfirmed` /
     `record_exit_realized_pnl` with `resp={"id": "?"}`, and drops the claim.

   The `reason_code` and `detail` are never logged, which is exactly why a
   total execution outage produced no error line in 9,217 log lines.
   PostgreSQL is up and healthy (`nervous-system-execution-postgres-1`, up 12
   days, port 55432 listening), so `PREFLIGHT_REFUSED` is the leading
   candidate.

   Blast radius: three real positions (Z, ZG, KEEL, ~$7,300 market value) are
   now unmanaged orphans; five closed_trade rows are fictitious; and the
   4 entries + 3 exits Meta deferred at 16:20 will be replayed through the same
   path at tomorrow's pre-open flush. **Fix before the 09:35 flush, or Meta
   will silently disown three more positions.**

2. **The SPY live loop ran the entire session at 100% saturation.**
   From 09:50 to 16:12 ET every `loop cost` line reported `busy=100%` (95-99%
   in the first two windows). Single SPY 10-minute bars took 227-1,318 seconds
   to process; the pending bar queue climbed from 39 at 09:50 to a peak of 271
   at 12:18 and only drained after 15:26. The `intraday-structure` subscriber
   on `SharedBarStream` hit its 50,000-slot queue cap 69 times and **dropped
   42,547 bars of 270,441 delivered (13.6%)**.

   Consequence: the SPY daytrader's decision loop was running 4-22 minutes
   behind the tape. A policy whose entry gate is a *1-minute* confirmation

   hour late. Yesterday's report left open whether SPY's silence was correct
   filtering or a silent break; this is the answer, and it is the latter.

   Caveat: this `loop cost` instrumentation appears in the uncommitted
   `UI/live_dashboard.py` and produced no lines before today, so we cannot say
   whether the saturation is new or newly visible.

3. **A new cross-module leak path: disowned-but-filled entries.**
   At 10:44:50 ET Intraday Structure tried to cancel what it believed was an
   unfilled entry and got `422 order is already in "filled" state`, then logged
   `entry for SPY260902C00765000 did not fill; not claiming it`. The account

   claim" path (`strategies/intraday_structure/execution.py`) needs to treat a
   `422 already filled` cancel rejection as proof the position IS held, not as
   confirmation that it isn't.

## Fixes Applied (2026-09-04)

In the working tree. **A server restart is required for any of it to take
effect** — and the server is currently stopped (it died at 2026-09-04 00:09 ET,
the recurring WSL2 pattern), so a restart picks all of this up at once.

1. **The Meta expiry window is now a trading-session window.**
   `MetaGatewayRouter._expires_at` runs to the end of the session after the
   decision bar's own session, replacing the flat 20-minute TTL. That is the
   same rule `core.live_4h_exec.pending_entry_bar_is_stale` already applies to
   the deferral queue, so the two guards agree instead of contradicting each
   other, it spans weekends and holidays without a magic number, and it stays a
   pure function of the bar — so the request hash and the deterministic client
   order ID are still stable across retries.

2. **A gateway that does not submit is no longer reported as a fill.**
   `coordinator._plan_and_maybe_submit` derives `submitted` from the
   `ExecutionResult` instead of hardcoding `True`. A new `gateway_verdict`
   helper in `live_runner` classifies a routed row three ways, because the
   middle case had no handling at all:
   - `ACCEPTED` — use the broker order ID.
   - `NO_EXPOSURE` (REFUSED / REJECTED) — restore a would-be exit, drop a
     would-be entry, log the `reason_code` and `detail` at error.
   - `UNCERTAIN` (DUPLICATE / AMBIGUOUS / RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED) — the broker
     may hold it, so **keep the claim**, book no fill, and say so loudly.
     Releasing a claim here is how a real position becomes an orphan.

3. **A placeholder order ID is treated as no ID.** `core.live_4h_exec`
   `broker_order_id()` rejects `"?"`/`""`/`"none"`, so the fill polls stop
   asking the broker about an order named `?` and silently returning
   `realized_pnl: null`.

4. **A cancel rejected as "already filled" is believed.**
   `IntradayOptionExecutor._cancel_quietly` treats a `422 order is already in
   "filled" state` as positive evidence: it re-reads the order, falls back to
   the account position clamped to the requested size, and — when the account
   cannot be read at all — claims the requested size rather than abandoning a
   position the broker just said exists.

5. **Swing's ownership veto fails closed.** `_sibling_module_owned_symbols`
   returns `None` when a sibling's book cannot be read, and `sync_from_broker`
   adopts nothing that pass (`sibling_ownership_unknown`). It used to return an
   empty set, i.e. "nobody owns anything", which is the answer most likely to
   cause an adoption. Backed by `claimed_symbols(strict=True)` and a new
   `ClaimBookUnreadable`; the orphan *reporter* stays lenient, since
   over-reporting is the safe direction for a detector that only warns.

6. **Claim books are read with a retry.** `core.orphan_positions._load_json_book`
   retries a failed parse three times with backoff, so a read landing mid-write
   stops being an ownership answer. A missing file still means "claims nothing";
   only an unparseable one raises. Also fixed: `state_paths={}` silently fell
   back to the live repo paths because of an `or` default.

7. **No more same-bar round trips.** The engine no longer calls
   `manage_running_setup` on the bar it entered on — the order is really sent
   after that bar closed, so judging the position against that bar's own
   high/low stopped it out on price action from before the fill. And
   `max_setup_bars` (how long an unentered setup may wait) no longer doubles as
   a position exit; a setup that confirms at or past the staleness limit is
   refused outright ("setup too old to enter") instead of being bought and
   dumped two seconds later.

8. **Unaffordable option entries are skipped, not submitted.**
   `filter_option_entries_by_buying_power` reads options buying power once,
   spends it in rank order, and records
   `insufficient_options_buying_power` in the plan audit. The cost-basis
   arithmetic reproduces Alpaca's own figures from the 09-02 rejections exactly
   (3 × 16.75 → $5,025.00 against its `cost_basis` of 5025.01). An unreadable
   account submits the plan unfiltered — this saves a doomed round trip, it is
   not a gate that can block a fundable order.

9. **Per-phase timing on the SPY bar handler.** `_timed_phase` wraps
   `policy.on_15m_bar`, `inference.on_15m_close`, `policy.on_decision`,
   `snapshot_broker_state` and the policy-cache persist, warning when any takes
   over 5 seconds. This does not fix the stall; it is what will name it.
