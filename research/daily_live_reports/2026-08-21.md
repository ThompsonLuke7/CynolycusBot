**Correction (added 2026-08-22): the broker's option `current_price` is the
BID, not the mid.** Verified by pulling live quotes for all 12 open option
contracts and comparing: in 12 of 12 the snapshot's `current_price` matches the
bid to within two cents (e.g. SPGI 5.50 vs bid 5.48 / ask 8.75). Because
entries fill above the mid and are then marked at the bid, every option
position books the full bid-ask spread as an unrealized loss the moment it
opens. Open-option values below are therefore **liquidation marks, not fair
values** — the honest answer to "what would I get if I sold right now", and a
substantial overstatement of economic loss. Where it changes a conclusion the
mid-mark equivalent is given alongside. Equity marks are unaffected.

## Operations And Bugs

1. **The nervous-system policy engine vetoes 100% of governed submissions, and
   the Meta Ranker is the only module wired to it.** The Postgres container
   `nervous-system-execution-postgres-1` is up and healthy, but the schema is
   effectively empty — `state_records` holds 4 rows and `policy_decisions`,
   `trade_intents`, `submission_attempts`, `order_requests` and every other
   execution table hold 0. With no ticker/liquidity state,
   `liquidity_vetoes` returns `LIQUIDITY_TICKER_STATE_MISSING` and
   `snapshot_vetoes` returns `SNAPSHOT_INVALID` plus the stale/missing pair. The
   engine is doing exactly what it is written to do; nothing is feeding it.
   This matches the open item recorded in `LIVING_SUMMARY.md` on 2026-08-19:
   the `meta_4h_1420@1` profile requires TICKER/MARKET/SECTOR/PORTFOLIO/READINESS
   state and no production code writes them; 2 of 5 were wired that evening,
   leaving the snapshot and liquidity rules failing. **This is the top-priority
   fix — it has now blocked meta entries and exits for two consecutive
   sessions.**
2. **Stale entry in the exit queue.** `pending_exit_orders.json` was last written
   at 09:35 ET and still lists `sell 1 MRVL260821C00210000`. That position was
   fully closed at 15:46 and the contract expired today. Monday's pre-open flush
   will attempt an exit against a position that does not exist.
3. **The data-readiness stamp was never refreshed for this session.** The 8/20
   22:15 readiness job was killed by the 23:45 server restart, and the restart's
   own catch-up was refused: `Data readiness: skipped (combined-server-data-
   readiness: another heavy data job is already running)` — the 23:45 nightly
   rerun held the heavy-job lock. Result: the 14:20 meta run skipped 5 live
   entries on the readiness gate. The 22:15 run tonight completed exit=0 at
   22:56 and the stamp is now OK (0.0h old). The gate needs a retry after the
   heavy-job lock clears rather than a single skipped attempt.
4. **Swing 30m is selecting contracts whose spreads exceed its edge.** Across
   490 multi-rung entry ladders in the audit history the median quoted spread is
   **12.1% of mid** and 63% exceed 10%, while the live gate
   (`signal_policy.max_entry_spread_pct_mid`) sits at **18%** and blocks almost
   nothing. Cumulative fill-versus-mid slippage is **$22,025** over 492 entries,
   **$10,749 of it in August alone**. That is the recurring, recoverable cost;
   the bid-mark artifact above is not. See the plan for the gate/ladder options.
5. **CRDO stopped out and re-entered 35 minutes later at a higher price.**
   -$2,380 realized at 13:50, then 3 fresh contracts at 17.00 at 14:25. Needs a
   same-session re-entry cooldown after a stop, keyed on the contract or the
   underlying.
6. **Two more submitted-but-unfilled orders logged as `OK buy`.** Momentum's
