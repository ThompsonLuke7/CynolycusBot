## Addendum — 2026-08-26: found while fixing

One defect below was not visible from the logs. It surfaced only when the
governed path was exercised directly, and it is recorded here because it was
armed on this session's queued orders.

**Governed option entries were sized in shares of the underlying, not
contracts.** `gateway_execution._route_one` sized every entry as
`shares_for_notional(underlying_price, budget)` with no option branch, then
handed that number to `option_order_request` as a **contract** count. The two
differ by roughly `(underlying / premium) * 100`. Measured against the four
option entries this session actually planned, at the $5,000 target notional:

| Contract | Planned | Correct | Router would have sent | Premium committed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| MRNA260918C00170000 (momentum, 14:00 bar) | 5 | 5 | 32 | $33,184 |
| MRNA260918C00150000 (meta, queued 8/24) | 5 | 5 | 36 | $34,452 |
| KSS260918C00017500 (meta + HTF, queued) | 24 | 24 | 283 | $58,015 |
| RBRK260918C00100000 (meta + HTF, queued) | 9 | 9 | 54 | $30,348 |

Six to twelve times the intended size. It never fired because only Meta routes
through the governed path and Meta has submitted nothing for four sessions —
the bugs in items 1-3 of Operations And Bugs were masking it. Fixing those
without fixing this would have unmasked it directly onto the entries queued for
the 8/26 open.
