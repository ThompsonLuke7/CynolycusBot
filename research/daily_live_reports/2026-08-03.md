## Operations And Bugs

1. **Clean shutdown at 21:21:32 ET, but the 22:15 ET data-readiness job is
   lost.** The server ran continuously from 09:15 ET with no watchdog restarts,
   no OOM, and RSS flat around 2.5-2.6 GB. It then received a shutdown signal at
   21:21:32 and stopped cleanly at 21:21:50. Because `--data-readiness-time
   22:15` lives in the now-dead scheduler, tonight's bars/HTF/meta matrix
   refresh will not run. The startup check tomorrow should catch up, but this
   should be confirmed rather than assumed.

2. **Nightly pipeline survived the shutdown and is still running.**
   `nightly_market_data.sh` (PID 1155659) launched at 16:45 ET and was still in
   its earnings-calendar stage at 21:28 ET, detached from the server. That is the
   desired behaviour, but it means the nightly's own completion is unsupervised
   tonight.

3. **Two-sleeve shadow tracker mis-priced every option-routed position.**
   *(Corrected 2026-08-03 22:5x ET after root-causing — the original wording here
   called all 227 exits fabricated, which overstated it. Detail below is the
   verified version; fix applied the same evening.)*

   `evaluate_exit` walks the UNDERLYING's 4H bars, but the entry price it was
   handed came from the managed position's `entry_avg_price`, which for
   option-routed positions is the OPTION PREMIUM. Comparing a $2-32 premium
   against a $15-532 share price made every option position clear the harvest
   sleeve's +7% target on its first bar. Measured against real bars: WDC implied
   +1599%, BE +915%, MLTX +831%, CIFR +701%, FCEL +652%, BLZE +588%.

   Scope: 6 of 227 recorded exits, all from today — but that is 6 of 6 option
   entries, and all three 4H modules now route to options, so the rate was about
   to become the norm. Equity-routed exits (221 of 227) used a correct share
   price and stand.

   Separately, and *not* a bug: all 227 exits being `target` at exactly +0.07 is
   the harvest policy's definition — it books its target on a target exit, and
   with target +7% against stop -59% on 4H bars, the target is nearly always
   what hits first. The constant is expected; the option mispricing was not.

   **Fixed:** entry price is now resolved route-aware from
   `order_audit.underlying_price`, with a scale-plausibility guard that skips
   rather than fabricating an exit. The 6 contaminated keys were retracted in the
   audit (`shadow_exit_retracted`) and re-entered at correct prices; none exited
   on the following run.

4. **Meta CRWV take-profit blocked by the OPG window.** The 16:20 ET sell of 12
   CRWV shares (`take_profit_+30%`) returned HTTP 403 with
   `opg orders must be submitted after 7:00pm and before 9:28am`. The position was
   restored to managed state and remains open at 75 shares. The 4H runners fire
   after the close but submit market orders that the broker only accepts in the
   OPG window; entries already handle this via `pending_open_entries.json`, but
   exits do not have the equivalent deferral path.

5. **NUL-byte corruption in a live audit log.** Line 709 of
   `Data/inference/momentum_expansion/live_signal_audit.jsonl` is a run of
   `\x00` bytes instead of JSON. Every reader must currently tolerate unparseable
   lines. This is the signature of an unclean process kill mid-write and is
   consistent with the known WSL2 crash history; the file is otherwise intact
   (904 of 905 lines parse).

6. **Underlying-based trailing stops are exiting option positions badly (CALM).**
   CALM was restored at 09:10 ET showing broker unrealized +$3,335 (+61.7%, option
   at 4.54 vs 2.35 basis). The underlying trail floor at 87.364 fired at 09:46
   when CALM rallied to 88.86. The underlying was still +3.56% in the position's
   favour, but the option had decayed to 2.20 — a realized **-6.38% / -$345**,
   giving back the entire +$3,335 gain. Worse, the exit went into a quote of
   bid 2.23 / ask 4.12 (spread 59.5% of mid) with a 1.76 limit. Two separate
   problems: the trail is measured on the wrong instrument, and there is no
   spread gate on exits (there is one on entries — 15 entries were skipped today
   for `entry_spread_too_wide`).

7. **14 swing buys exhausted their verification ladders.** MKSI, AEIS, UMC, BP,

8. **SPY daytrader exit latency.** The short close was decided on the 15:01 ET

    live inference path. It is now confirmed to fail in the nightly path too, so
    theme-derived features stay frozen at their 2026-07-21 outputs.
