### What was actually wrong

The Fix Pass above described two symptoms. Their causes turned out to be four
separate bugs, all in how positions are tracked.

1. **A new position kept its first partial-fill reading as its size**
   (`core/live_4h_exec.py`, all four 4H modules and the Dealer Ranker).
   - The order is read about a second after it is sent. MYGN was ordered at
     1,264 shares, read at 51, and filled in full moments later. State said 51.
   - Sells are capped at the recorded size, so the exit sells 51 and leaves
     1,213 shares that no module owns. This is where the unowned leftovers came
     from.
   - 56 positions were short of the broker this way on 10-05. Another 15 Meta
     positions were over (planned size kept from before fills were read).
   - Fix: when an entry settles, its order is read again and the size set to
     what it filled (`settle_entry_fill`).
2. **Intraday Structure dropped its own fresh positions**
   (`core/order_reconciliation.py`).
   - It keys positions by setup id and reuses ids across days. Exit recovery
     told positions apart by `entry_bar`, which its records do not carry, so
     any two trades under one id looked identical.
   - On 10-05 a BAC call bought at 11:36 was matched to the **10-01** sale of
     a different BAC contract, declared closed, and dropped while still held.
     GOOGL and both QQQ setups went the same way (matched to 09-29, 09-21 and
     09-25 orders).
   - This is the real cause of bug 3. The 30m swing then adopted and sold
     contracts that looked unowned.
   - Fix: a saved exit is only re-attached to the position it came from,
     identified by its entry order (`same_position`).
3. **The 30m swing wiped its own book while restoring it**
   (`strategies/multi_ticker_swing/live/position_manager.py`).
   - Restoring the first position at startup rewrote the saved book with only
     that position. Every other position lost its record, came back as
     "unknown" with a synthetic entry price, and was loss-cut at the open.
   - That is the `restored_unknown_loss_cut` line in this report: six closes
     and -$12,903 in the week, attributed above to the nightly restarts.
   - Fix: the book is written once, after every position has been restored.
4. **The 30m swing refused its own contract when a sibling held the shares.**
   - PENG261016C00060000, bought 09-30, was refused 1,547 times on 10-01
     alone because HTF holds PENG shares.
   - Fix: a contract in the swing's own book is restored regardless. A second
     guard now re-checks sibling claims before the swing acts on any contract
     it merely found, and releases it without an order if a sibling claims it.

Also fixed: **Meta option entries** (bug 2). The broker adapter sent single
option orders through Alpaca's multi-leg endpoint. It now sends them as
ordinary orders on the contract
(`core/nervous_system/execution/alpaca_adapter.py`).
