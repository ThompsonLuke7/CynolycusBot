## Operations And Bugs

1. **[High, ledger] Meta equity exits booked as options (x100).**
   - `signals/meta_context/meta_ranker/live_runner.py:774` calls
     `_submit_via_gateway(..., is_option=True)` for the whole mixed
     option/equity plan.
   - Line 1195 then tags every sell `"option" if is_option`.
   - Since `5b716b8` (09-21) that item flows into
     `core/live_4h_exec.track_exit_submission`, which multiplies P&L and
     proceeds by 100.
   - Affected: EQPT 09-24 (-$90,611 recorded vs -$906.11 true), ACHR 09-28
     (-$3,848 vs -$38.48), CAVA 09-29 (-$78,560 vs -$785.60).
   - The same flag sets `state["contracts"]` instead of `shares`, so a partial
     Meta equity fill would leave a stale share count.
   - Fix: take route from the row (OCC or not), not from the plan flag. Also
     remove the `"option"` defaults at `core/live_4h_exec.py:1969` and `:2004`.
   - The raw ledger rows were left untouched; they need a documented repair
     row.
2. **[High, execution] Meta option entries always rejected.**
   - `core/nervous_system/execution/alpaca_adapter.py:245-248` sends every
     non-equity request to `_submit_multileg`. A single long call is a 1-leg
     "mleg", and Alpaca refuses those.
   - Seen 09-14 (2), 09-21 (1), 09-22 (7), 09-24 (6), 09-28 (3), 09-29 (2).
   - Fix needs a single-leg option submit branch. Decide first whether you
     want Meta option entries back on, given the route evidence.
3. **[High, ownership] 30m swing adopts Intraday Structure positions.**
   - On 09-29, QQQ260929P00735000 was bought by Intraday Structure at 11:06 and
     12:28. The 30m swing position manager logged `OPEN dir=-1` about 40
     seconds later and closed it as `restored_unknown_expiring` within 1-3
     minutes.
   - It happened only on QQQ, only on 09-29, in this window. It will recur
     whenever Intraday trades an index ETF the 30m swing also watches.
4. **[Medium] Stale Meta NTSK expired option** (open since 09-18). It needs a
