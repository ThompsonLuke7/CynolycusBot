## Operations And Bugs

1. **Stale readiness stamp blanked an entire trading day (highest priority).**
   `Data/readiness/latest_success.json` is dated `2026-07-28T02:05:48Z`
   (2026-07-27 22:05 ET). The gate in `core/live_readiness.py:76` requires the
   stamp to postdate the last completed session's 16:00 ET close, so on 7/29 it
   rejected all 28 entries. The mechanism:
   - `scripts/nightly_data_readiness.sh:149` is the only writer of the stamp.
     The nightly *market data* pipeline does not write it.
   - The 22:15 ET readiness slot was missed on 7/28 because the server launched
     at 22:30 ET, after the slot had passed.
   - `_data_readiness_startup_check()` (`UI/combined_server.py:240`) then
     evaluated the stamp against **7/28's** threshold (`prev_trading_day` =
     7/27, required after 7/27 16:00 ET), which the 7/27 22:05 stamp satisfied,
     and logged *"stamp is current at startup — no catch-up needed"*. Correct
     for that instant, wrong for the next session.
   - The 22:15 ET slot on 7/29 never fired because the server was dead by then.

   The check asks "does the stamp authorize entries now?" when it needs to ask
   "will it still authorize entries at the next session's open, given the next
   scheduled readiness run?" A boot in the window between the readiness slot and
   midnight silently guarantees a dark next day. That docstring already cites
   2026-07-24 as a prior occurrence of the same dark-day outcome, so this is at
   least the second instance.

   **The stamp is still stale right now, and nothing is running.** Unless the
   readiness job is run before 7/30's open, 7/30 will be dark too.

2. **Silent server death at ~18:53 ET with no restart.** Last server-log line
   18:48:28 ET; last write anywhere was the SPY daytrader's
   `broker-state.jsonl` at 18:53:02 ET. Evidence gathered:
   - No `Shutdown signal received`, no SIGTERM/SIGKILL, no traceback.
   - **No OOM.** The memory keeper reported a flat RSS of 2,533-2,536 MB for the
     three hours before death; the most recent OOM kills in the WSL crash log
     are from Jul 2 and Jul 6; 17.6 GB RAM and 23.8 GB swap are free now.
   - **No machine reboot** — host boot was 2026-07-27 20:00 ET, uptime 2d 2h.
   - Critically, the **supervisor died at the same moment**: there is no
     `[supervisor] combined_server exited rc=... — restarting` line, so the
     restart ladder never engaged. The whole process group vanished at once.
   - Orphaned children survived: the heartbeat watchdog (PID 603114) and two
     `dmesg --follow` crash loggers are still running, and the nightly job ran
     to completion. The heartbeat watchdog only alerts and has `webhook=off`,
     so it did nothing observable.

   This resembles the known WSL2 crash pattern but without the usual reboot.
   The supervisor being inside the blast radius is the actionable part — a
   supervisor that dies with its child cannot restart it.

3. **Market-regime data is 6 days stale.** 5,471 warnings from
   `strategies.momentum_expansion.features.feature_matrix_4h`: *"most recent 4H
   bar's joined market-regime row is 6 days stale (> 5 day tolerance) —
   daily_regime.parquet likely needs a rebuild (`python -m
   signals.market_regime.build`)"*. Scoring proceeded on stale context with
   `regime_stale_days=6` exposed as a feature, so Momentum's 7/29 rankings were
   produced against week-old regime data. Handled honestly by the code, but the
   rebuild is overdue.

4. **One SPY round trip is missing from the trade-events audit.** The broker
   fill stream shows SPY260729P00740000 bought at 13:14:59 ET and sold at
   13:29:01 ET (-$2), and `decision-10m.jsonl` shows the matching

   `open_short`/`close_short` pair for it. Conversely, trade-events logs an
