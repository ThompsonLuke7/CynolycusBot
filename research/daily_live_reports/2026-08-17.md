## Operations And Bugs

1. **The 4H risk pass has never run a tick.** `RiskPassSupervisor.run` calls
   `datetime.now(tz)` in a module that never imported `datetime`, so every wake
   raised `NameError` and was swallowed by the `except Exception` in the loop —
   52 tracebacks between 12:04:41 and 16:25:52 ET. Commit `e1aa3f6` fixed the
   import at 13:44 ET, but the running process had already loaded the old code,
   so the fix did not take effect until the 16:27:52 restart — by which time the
   market was closed and the 09:30-16:00 guard kept it idle. The supervisor was
   introduced in the nervous-system merge over the weekend (it does not appear
   at all in `server_20260812.log`), so **stop and expiry management has not
   executed once since it was added.** First real test is tomorrow's open.
2. **Nothing ran from 23:38 Sunday to 12:04 Monday.** The prior server was shut
   down deliberately on 08-16 and was not restarted before the open. The cost is
   concrete: the 09:35 and 09:37 pre-open flush windows never fired.
3. **Deferred entries from 2026-08-14 are still unflushed.** Momentum's
   `pending_open_entries.json` has not been written since 08-14 20:31 UTC and
   still holds OUST260821C00050000 (22 contracts) against a 08-14 bar. Meta
   carries RBRK and HTF carries five names from the same bar. These needed the
   pre-open flush that item 2 killed, and Momentum's file is now three sessions
   stale.
4. **Friday's ITM calls auto-exercised into equity.** Four 08-14 expiries were
   held to expiration and assigned: GRAB 238 contracts -> 23,800 shares
   ($85,204), U 17 -> 1,700 shares ($77,010), SMCI 11 -> +1,100 shares, FIG 31
   -> +2,901 shares. HPE and ZM expired worthless. Buying power fell from
   $1,419,497 to $929,309 (-$490,189) and cash from -$333,748 to -$636,850. No
   module currently manages these equity positions. This predates the risk pass
   supervisor, so item 1 is not the cause — but expiry management is exactly
   what the supervisor is supposed to prevent this with.
   **Action taken 22:50 ET:** four `close_position` entries queued on the paper
   account via `core.startup_queue` — GRAB all 23,800 sh, U all 1,700 sh, SMCI
   1,075 of 1,232, FIG 2,864 of 3,100, ~$274,693 notional. SMCI and FIG are
   partial by design: Meta's managed state claims 157 SMCI shares and HTF's
   claims 236 FIG, so only the exercised delta is sold. They drain at the next
   boot or the 09:35 ET market-hours loop and submit as market orders.
5. **`restored_unknown_loss_cut` fires on marks it admits are unusable.** On
   restore, 12 of 15 swing positions came back with
   `pnl_pct_source: restore_time_mark_unusable`, a synthetic `entry_price` equal
   to last price, and `pnl_pct: null`. The defensive rule then cut DD and PM on
   `broker_unrealized_plpc` alone. The losses were real, but the module
   re-entered PM 1h38m later, which suggests the cut was state-loss-driven
   rather than signal-driven.
6. **The close-order limit ladder almost never fills.** DD walked 3.99 -> 3.84
   -> 3.69 -> 3.54 -> 3.39 across five attempts, every one `not verified
   via=timeout`, before a market fallback filled it. Across the session only 1
   close verified on a limit and 1 needed the market fallback. Each rung costs
   ~4s and a cancel/resubmit round trip.
7. **Worth checking: the open-side retry ladder may not advance its limit.** At
   17:52 UTC the SPY daytrader logged `next_limit=1.35` on four consecutive
   retries but resubmitted at `limit_price=1.34` every time, burning all five
   attempts at an unchanged price. The 18:51 UTC open did advance (1.46 ->
   1.47), so this is not conclusive — the limit may be re-derived from a fresh
   mid each attempt and have legitimately landed on 1.34. Flagging for a look,
   not asserting a defect. Cancels were correctly issued between attempts, so
   there was no duplicate-fill risk.
8. **Watchdog HUNG alert at 12:23:50 ET** — "no audit write for 8m during RTH".
   The server had started 19 minutes earlier and was in warmup, and it continued
   normally, so this is consistent with a startup false positive rather than a

9. **`Data/inference/intraday_structure/state.json` is 94 MB.** Not failing, but
