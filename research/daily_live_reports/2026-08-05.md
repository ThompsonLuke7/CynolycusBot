## Operations And Bugs

1. **VALE was liquidated at $0.01 into a 0.01 x 0.74 market — roughly $2,870 of
   avoidable loss.** The position (159 contracts of VALE260828C00015000) was
   restored from the broker at 08:31 with `broker_unrealized_plpc = -0.40625`,
   so `restored_unknown_loss_cut` correctly fired at 09:34. The exit then failed
   four times because the bid was 0.00: three ladder cycles (limits 0.17, 0.15,
   0.15) each exhausted 5 attempts and each fell through to a market fallback
   that returned `403 ... no available quote for symbol. please reenter with a
   limit`. On the fourth cycle at 09:49 the bid ticked to 0.01, the pricing
   source switched to `bid`, `base_limit=0.01` was submitted, and it filled
   instantly. Broker market value at the time was $3,021 (mark 0.19) against a
   $5,088 cost basis. The close-pricing ladder has **no floor relative to mid or
   mark** — when the bid is a penny it will sell the whole position for a penny.
   This is the highest-value fix available right now.

2. **The same VALE record shows the restored-position P&L accounting is wrong.**
   The audit logged `pnl_pct: 0.0` and `option_entry_price: 0.32` (the current
   mark) on every event, including the defensive-exit trigger, while the broker
   reported -40.6% on the same position in the same payload
   (`option_entry_meta.broker_unrealized_plpc`). The exit fired off the broker
   number, correctly — but anything downstream reading the module's own
   `pnl_pct` for a restored position is reading zero when the truth is -40%.

3. **Both HTF exits at the 16:25 run failed and one position is stuck
   overnight.** `SELL 16 AEVA shares @market [take_profit_+30%]` returned
   `403 opg orders must be submitted after 7:00pm and before 9:28am`, and
   `SELL 43 CLSK260821C00015000 @market [stop_-39%]` returned
   `422 options market orders are only allowed during market hours`. Only CLSK
   logged `exit submit failed ... restoring to managed state`; AEVA did not, so
   the two failures are handled by different paths. Net effect: AEVA is still
   100 shares at +42.6% untrimmed, and the CLSK call sits at -42.5% with its
   stop unfired.

   **Corrected diagnosis (added after review).** My first read — that the
   after-close deferral covers entries only — was wrong. `core.live_4h_exec`
   already has `defer_exits_if_opg_unavailable` (the AEVA case) and
   `submit_option_exit_with_ladder` (the CLSK case). Neither ran because **HTF
   and Meta do not use the shared executor**: Momentum calls `execute_plan` and
   gets both, while HTF and Meta each carry a copy-pasted local `_execute` that
   wires only the *entry* deferral. The 2026-08-03 fix landed in one of three
   modules, which is exactly why entries deferred correctly today and exits did
   not. Fixed the same evening — see the 22:16 LIVING_SUMMARY entry.

4. **A SPY daytrader exit failed to fill and cost about $32.** The 11:56 close of
   SPY260805C00773000 exhausted its ladder, went to `PENDING RECONCILE` with a
   600s grace, and was canceled at 12:09:51 with the position still open. It was
   not retried until the next signal at 13:30, by which time the contract had
   fallen 0.73 -> 0.41. A canceled exit that leaves a live position should
   re-arm on the next bar, not wait for a fresh signal.
