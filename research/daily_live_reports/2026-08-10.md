## Operations And Bugs

1. **TENX and SION repriced ~10x lower over the weekend with share counts
   unchanged; the system read it as a -91% drawdown and stopped out, realizing
   -$6,029.** This has the signature of an unadjusted forward stock split, not a
   market move.

   Evidence:
   - 08-07 close: TENX 100sh @ 13.30, SION 105sh @ 49.61 (broker snapshot).
     08-10 fills: TENX @ 1.42, SION @ 4.46. Ratios 9.37x and 11.12x.
   - Divide Friday's close by exactly 10 and both become ordinary days: TENX
     1.33 -> 1.42 (+6.8%), SION 4.96 -> 4.46 (-10.1%).
   - In a 173-name equity book, the worst other move today was BTDR at -18.9%.
     Two unrelated microcaps posting -89% and -91% on the same session, both
     landing within a normal day's move of exactly one-tenth of Friday's close,
     is not a market event.
   - The broker did **not** adjust share counts: selling 100 TENX and 105 SION
     fully closed both positions. A correctly processed 10:1 split would have
     left ~1,000 / ~1,050 shares.
   - The module's own decision price agreed with the fill (`decision_gain`
     -0.9111 / -0.9069), so this is not a stale-mark problem — the price feed
     itself carried the split-adjusted price against an unadjusted cost basis.
   - `grep -rE "split_(ratio|factor|adjust)|corporate_action"` across `core/`,
     `strategies/`, `signals/` and `UI/` returns **nothing**. There is no
     corporate-action handling anywhere in the live path.

   This is exactly the failure mode `AGENTS.md` calls out under data integrity
   ("handle corporate actions, delistings, symbol changes, splits ... explicitly
   where relevant"). Impact is not limited to P&L reporting: any split in a held
   name will trip `stop_-39%` on the next 4H bar and liquidate the position at
   what the system thinks is a -90% loss. Suggested fix: reject a bar-over-bar
   gap beyond a plausible threshold (say -70%) as a data event rather than a
   signal, require a corporate-action confirmation before acting, and reconcile
   cost basis against broker `avg_entry_price` before evaluating stops.
   **Recommend confirming both corporate actions against an external source
   before accepting the -$6,029 as realized.**

2. **Dealer Ranker crashed on a logging f-string and submitted zero orders —
   second consecutive session with no orders, new root cause.** At
   `core/live_4h_exec.py:283`, inside the `if verbose:` block:

   ```python
   f"delta={order.get('delta'):.2f} mid={order['mid']:.2f}  oi={order.get('open_interest')}"
   ```

   `order.get('delta')` returned `None` for the third selected contract, raising
   `TypeError: unsupported format string passed to NoneType.__format__`. The
   traceback propagates through `build_mixed_plan` -> `main` -> `SystemExit`, so
   the whole run died at 15:53:45 ET. UMC 20C x60 and GFI 40C x18 had already
   been selected and printed; neither was submitted.

   Two distinct defects here. First, a *diagnostic print* is inside the
   transaction path and can abort order submission — no logging statement should
   be able to do that. Second, a contract with `delta=None` passed the delta
   filter at all, which means the selection logic accepted a contract it could
   not price. Suggested fix: guard the format (`delta if delta is not None`), and
   separately reject `delta is None` contracts during selection rather than
   letting them reach the plan.
