## Operations And Bugs

1. ~~**A triggered stop was not executed.**~~ **RETRACTED 2026-08-14 — this was
   not a bug; the original finding was wrong.** The claim was that momentum's
   16:30 ET `AAOI260821C00140000 stop_-39%` sell had been dropped by the
   after-close deferral path. It had not. The runner logged, at 16:30:59:

       momentum_expansion: exit for AAOI (AAOI260821C00140000) deferred —
         restoring to managed state so the position stays claimed until the
         pre-open flush
       momentum_expansion: market closed — deferred 1 exits to next open
         (1 option, 0 equity) -> .../pending_exit_orders.json

   Exits defer through `pending_exit_orders.json`, a separate file from
   `pending_open_entries.json`; I only checked the latter and concluded from its
   absence that the sell was lost. The deferred sell flushed at 09:43:33 ET on
   08-14 and filled at **9.90 for +$350**, a profit. `defer_exits_if_opg_unavailable`
   and `core/tests/test_pending_exit_deferral.py` already cover this path.

   What is real, and was hidden underneath the wrong claim: the **16:30 decision
   mark was badly wrong**. The stop fired on a mark of 4.70 against a 9.20 basis
   (-48.9%); the contract actually traded at 9.90 the next morning, so the true
   position was **+7.6%**, and the ledger recorded `stop_overshoot` of **+0.466**
   — a positive overshoot, meaning the exit was better than the threshold by
   that much, not worse. A stop fired on a stale/one-sided after-hours option
   quote. That belongs with the entry-pricing finding in item 2: both are the
   same underlying issue of trusting a wide-spread option quote as a mark. Every
   open-mark figure in this report that comes from an extended-hours snapshot
   carries the same caveat.

   The negative overshoots quoted elsewhere in this report (LITE -0.471, HBM
   -0.400, LQDA -0.202, TEL -0.143) are unaffected and stand.
2. **Dealer Ranker entries are again deeply underwater within 13 minutes.** At
   the 16:05 ET snapshot, minutes after the 15:52 fills: ASX 1.50 -> 0.70
   (-53.3%), SGI 0.65 -> 0.35 (-46.2%), ZM 1.85 -> 1.31 (-29.2%), GRAB 0.19 ->
   0.18 (-5.3%). Median about -37%, worse than 8/12's -28%. Four of today's five
   dealer closes were -50% stops, three of them at `runs_held` 1 or 2. Until the
   fill-versus-quote question is resolved, dealer P&L should not be read as
   evidence about the dealer signal. Verify the mark source (bid vs mid vs last
   print) and the fill against the quoted spread first, per `AGENTS.md`.
3. **Trim/partial exits do not decrement the managed position size.** In
   [core/live_4h_exec.py:325-330](core/live_4h_exec.py#L325-L330) the `trim`
   branch appends a partial sell and sets `st["trimmed"] = True` but never
   reduces `st["contracts"]` / `st["shares"]`. Trading is unaffected — exit
   quantity is re-read from the broker each run at
   [core/live_4h_exec.py:261](core/live_4h_exec.py#L261) — but state-derived
   open-book valuations are overstated. Confirmed today: dealer FIG state 61
   contracts vs broker 31 (a $6,360 overstatement at the 2.92 mark); dealer P
   state 11 vs broker 5.
4. **Two dealer entries never reached the broker.** IONS260821C00055000 and
