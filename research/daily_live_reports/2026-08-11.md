## Operations And Bugs

1. **Deferred exits that flush at the next open produce no realized-P&L ledger
   row.** Both of 08-10's deferred exits filled this morning —
   `OK deferred-exit sell 2 AMAT260821C00550000` at 09:35:0x and
   `OK deferred-exit sell 19 VSH260821C00035000` at 09:37:0x, each with a broker
   order id and `pending-exit flush: submitted 1 / skipped 0`. Neither appears in
   any `closed_trades.jsonl`, and no `realized-PnL logged` line was emitted for
   either. Only the six same-run exits got ledger rows.

   Two consequences. First, today's reported realized P&L is understated by
   whatever those two closed at; against 08-10 closing marks they were worth
   roughly -$2,850 (AMAT) and -$1,520 (VSH), but **those are marks, not fills —
   the actual fills are not recorded anywhere I can find**, which is the defect.
   Second and more important: the stop-overshoot instrumentation added in the
   uncommitted `core/live_4h_exec.py` diff exists specifically to measure how far
   past the threshold a stop travels between tests, and gap-deferred exits are
   *the* population most exposed to that — they sit unpriced from the 16:20 ET
   decision to the 09:35 ET flush. The ledger is blind to exactly the cases it
   was built to measure. `record_exit_realized_pnl` needs to be called from the
   pending-exit flush path, not only from the in-run exit path.

2. **~~The SPY daytrader held a position overnight with no end-of-day
   flatten.~~ WITHDRAWN — this is by design, not a defect.** It bought 1x
   SPY260812C00770000 at 14:44 ET (2.29) and carried it into the close; the
   20:05 snapshot shows it at 2.38. I filed this as a missing flatten without
   first reading the flatten. `_maybe_force_close_expiring_positions` in
   `strategies/spy_intraday/Policy/order_policy.py` is deliberately scoped by
   `_symbol_expires_today`: it force-closes contracts expiring **that session**
   (the 0DTE leak it exists to prevent, with its own regression test in
   `strategies/spy_intraday/tests/test_expiring_flatten.py`) and deliberately
   leaves later expiries alone. SPY260812C00770000 expires 08-12, so the flatten
   correctly did not fire. The module ended flat on 08-07 and 08-10 by outcome,
   not by rule.

   What remains is a position question rather than a bug: the module is willing
   to carry overnight gamma on a 1DTE contract, which is worth an explicit
   decision, but nothing is broken and no fix is warranted.

3. **Dealer Ranker submits entries seven minutes before the close and 44% do not
   fill.** Its scheduled time is 15:45 ET (`--dealer-ranker-time 15:45`), the
   universe scan takes ~8 minutes, and the plan lands at 15:53. Four of nine
   limit entries (UMC 19C, P 110C, GRAB 3.5C, TSCO 36C) were accepted and never
   filled. Worse, all nine were written into `live_state.json` as `managed`, so
   the module now believes it holds 11 positions against the broker's 7 — it will
   evaluate stops and take-profits on four contracts it does not own. Either move
   the run earlier or reconcile `managed` against broker fills before persisting
   state. This is a milder cousin of the same "state written as though the
   order succeeded" pattern.

4. **A module-ownership race let swing 30m adopt an HTF position for 31
   minutes.** At 09:06:28 ET the swing 30m broker sync restored VSH as its own

   contracts. Swing detected `broker_position_missing` 31 seconds later and
