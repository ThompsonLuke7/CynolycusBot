The attribution is solid: the readiness log's final write (09:49:55) coincides
to the second with the memory spike, and `combined_server`'s own memory keeper
reported RSS of only 1,074–2,125 MB throughout — it appears as the ~1.1 GB
second entry in the top-RSS table, not the 17 GB first entry. The server was a
bystander.

The mechanism is plausible on inspection: `features_4h.parquet` is **4.37 GB on
disk** (`strategies/momentum_expansion/data/processed/`), and its combined-parquet
assembly is a whole-file read/rewrite. A 4.37 GB compressed parquet expanding
past 17 GB in memory is unsurprising. Note the file's mtime is **2026-07-27
21:50** — it has not been successfully rebuilt since. The same signature appears
overnight 07-30→07-31 (a python process ran 00:05–00:28 ET on the identical
5→17 GB sawtooth, with page-allocation failures at 00:28:37 and 00:28:54), so
this job has now taken the box down twice in 24 hours.

Note the kernel OOM killer never fired on 07-31 — a targeted kill would have been
the *better* outcome. Instead swap was consumed entirely and the VM livelocked,
taking every unrelated process with it. Grep of `dmesg.log` shows the last actual
`Killed process` events were 2026-07-02 and 2026-07-06.
