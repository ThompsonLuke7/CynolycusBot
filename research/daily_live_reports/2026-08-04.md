   commit), silently reverting every fix committed at 00:52. The server started
   at 09:25 from that tree, so it ran the whole session without: the RSI ticker
   filter, price-recap labelling, the shadow tracker option-price fix, the swing
   exit guards, the after-close OPG exit deferral, and the audit fsync. Nothing
   was lost — all of it was safely committed on `main`. Corrected at 16:56;
   branches consolidated to `main` / `gcp-migration` / `nervous-system-execution`
   and the server restarted on `main`.

2. **This also explains the readiness failures.** `main` carries a float32
   downcast whose own comment reads *"float64 build was OOM-killed above a 16 GB
   cap, the float32 build completes"* — the exact failure chased overnight. The
   07-30 tree lacked it, so stage 4 hit 18.9 GB RSS, livelocked the box once
   (killed manually at 01:34 to save the VM) and was OOM-killed under a 16 GiB
   cgroup cap at 02:13. A third run at 09:41, capped and nice'd, completed at
   **10:24:32, status=0** and stamped readiness.
