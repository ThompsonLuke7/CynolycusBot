## Operations And Bugs

1. **Nervous-system Postgres was down all session, and the Meta Ranker has no
   fallback.** `psycopg.OperationalError: connection to server at "127.0.0.1",
   port 55432 failed: Connection refused` appears 4 times. At 09:35 it failed 2
   exits cleanly; at 14:20 it propagated to `SystemExit` and destroyed a
   9-order plan. The governed path needs to either degrade to the direct broker
   path or write the plan to the pending files before the gateway call. The
   container `nervous-system-execution-postgres-1` came back up around 23:30 ET.
2. **A live entry decision was aged out and discarded.** `meta_ranker
   pending-open: EXPIRED buy PSIG x3378 from bar 2026-08-18 18:00 — decision is
   older than the last session, dropping (attempts=1)`. The expiry rule is
   right; it fired because the veto held the order for two sessions.
3. **Unconfirmed option entries are written into managed state.** The dealer
   ranker logged `OK buy` for MRNA260828C00140000 x7 and NEM260828C00130000 x18
   after `entry ladder ... unfilled ... left resting at the ask, entry stays
   unconfirmed`, added both to `live_state.json`, and only removed them
   ~24h later via `build_mixed_plan: dropping ... not_found`. For that window
   the module believed it held positions it did not own. Compare the swing 30m
   runner, which handles the same situation correctly by skipping the local open.
4. **Nightly market data timed out.** `ticker discovery exit=124` at 19:23:08
   after ~62 minutes, carrying the wrapper to `complete status=124` at 21:54:51.
   The 23:45:10 rerun completed status=0 at 04:34 on 8/21, so no data was
   permanently missed — but see the 8/21 report for the downstream effect on the
   readiness stamp.
5. **Silent server stop.** The 00:03 instance's last log line is 22:43:21 ET;
   the supervisor's next line is a fresh `starting combined_server
   (fast_fails=0)` at 23:45:04. No exit code, no watchdog stale-heartbeat alert,
   no OOM or exit-137 signature. Outside market hours, so no trading impact.
6. **A running data job was killed by that restart.** The 22:15 ET data-readiness
   job was launched at 22:15:00 and never logged a `finished (exit=...)` line.
   Its consequences land on 8/21.
7. **COST was entered against its own dealer gate.** The entry audit carries
   `dealer_gate: {action: veto, reason: call_wall_overhead}` and a 17.8%
   quoted spread, and the trade went on to be the day's largest loss. Worth
   checking whether the gate is advisory by design.
