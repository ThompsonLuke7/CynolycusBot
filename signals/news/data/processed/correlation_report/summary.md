## Reading guide
- `expansion_hit_rate` is the share of records whose max forward return within 10 days reached +10%.
- A cohort with `labeled_count < 20` should be ignored — t-stats are unstable.
- A cohort whose `tstat_fwd_5d` is significant in both `train_2023_2024` and `eval_2025_2026` is a genuinely useful filter; one that's only significant in train is overfit-by-luck.
- `news_similarity_score` is leak-free as of this build: priors only contribute when their 10-day label was realized by the prediction time. If you used the old artifact, results may have looked stronger than reality.
