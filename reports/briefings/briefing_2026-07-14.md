## 4H shares and the readiness breach

No 4H position closed. The 09:35/09:37 pre-open flush submitted 16 queued long-share entries:

- Meta: TENX, PURR, DGXX, QTTB, ANRO, PONY.
- HTF: ASST, LUNR, RCAT, HTZ, AOSL, INFQ, RGTI.
- Momentum: FBRX, NGNE, DMRA.

Those orders should have been blocked. `Data/readiness/latest_success.json` still records 2026-07-12 17:26:44 UTC, which predates the required 2026-07-13 16:00 ET session boundary. Normal 14:20/14:25/16:20/16:25 order plans correctly rejected entries, and Dealer Ranker later rejected eight more, proving the readiness rule itself worked.

The defect was a separate execution path: `submit_pending_open_entries()` reranked and sent queued orders without calling `filter_entry_orders_for_readiness()`. The fix now filters every eligible pending order before submission, logs the readiness reason, records each skip, and clears the unsafe queue. A focused regression test proves a stale stamp submits zero orders.

This fix affects future process launches; it cannot undo today's paper fills. No order was submitted, modified, or canceled during this investigation.
