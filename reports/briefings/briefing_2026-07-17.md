## Terminal errors and operational impact

### 1. Alpaca timeout loop — confirmed root cause of the pasted traceback

The stack ends in `urllib.request.urlopen()` while `UI/live_dashboard.py` calls SPY `reconcile_with_broker()` → `get_positions()`. This is an outbound broker-read timeout. It does not show a strategy exception, corrupt signal, rejected order, or process crash.

- First occurrence: **18:22:45 ET**.
- Last occurrence: **00:51:35 ET**.
- Count: **465 warning events** and 930 nested traceback headers, because each timeout prints both the socket and wrapped `urllib` trace.
- Direct route checks after the final warning returned fast HTTP 401 responses from Alpaca/Schwab without credentials, confirming network reachability had recovered.
- Impact: after-hours SPY reconciliation failed repeatedly; the **20:05 ET paper broker snapshot failed**; nightly Alpaca universe discovery also timed out.
- No observed trading impact: SPY was flat at 15:13, Swing's final actions were complete by 15:53, and the 16:05 close snapshot succeeded before the outage.

The retry behavior is too noisy and inefficient: it retries roughly every 30 seconds with a full traceback and no meaningful backoff/circuit breaker.

### 2. Nightly pipeline degraded and still incomplete at audit time

The nightly job started at 16:45 ET. Its second dealer-snapshot capture hit its four-hour limit (`exit=124`) at 21:44 after reaching 2,725/2,790 tasks; the earlier 15:45 Dealer Ranker snapshot remained available. FINRA fetch/compaction completed, but Alpaca broad-universe discovery timed out. Catalyst discovery and the universe rebuild continued from available data. Priority news collection started at 21:56 and was still running at 01:04 ET under its four-hour guard. No `nightly_market_data.sh complete` line existed at audit time.

### 3. State ownership/fill integrity defects

- **Phantom SONY:** `Data/inference/dealer_ranker/live_state.json` claims SONY as managed, but the authoritative broker snapshot has no SONY option position. The ranker is treating order acceptance/submission as ownership without verified fill.
- **AUR double ownership:** Dealer Ranker originated and owns AUR, but Swing's 15:53 broker reconciliation restored the same contract as `missing_local_state` and wrote it into `Data/inference/multi_ticker_swing/open_positions.json`. Both modules can now believe they are responsible for exits.
- **Momentum API latency:** `/api/state` on port 8770 did not answer within 20 seconds during the audit. Its snapshot path can synchronously perform account I/O and a full-universe evaluation on a cold cache, so dashboard polling can block even while the scheduled runner remains alive.

Short-lived `BrokenPipeError` lines at the end of the server log were caused by audit HTTP clients timing out/disconnecting while slow snapshots were still being assembled; they are not evidence of a trading failure.
