## SPY intraday diagnosis

All five trades were calls and all five exited through `setup_failure_exit`. SPY weakened from roughly 752.6 near the opening decision window to 749.1 at the final 10-minute bar. Loss containment worked—no individual loss exceeded $14 and no position remained open—but the model repeatedly re-entered the same losing direction.

Context availability was degraded. Across 39 action decisions, UUP was stale 19 times, lagging 14 times, and present only 6 times. At the first decision, every context series except QQQ was stale from the prior session. Other context series largely recovered, while UUP remained sparse for much of the day. The current system reports this condition but does not block entries on it.

This is enough evidence for a focused SPY replay/ablation, but not enough to retune thresholds from one losing day. The next defensible experiment is to replay 7/13 with UUP removed/fresh and compare call-entry frequency, then validate any freshness gate across the fixed historical test protocol.

Execution controls behaved correctly: 60 wide-spread entries were blocked, 14 unverified buy attempts did not create local positions, and final broker reconciliation was 11 broker / 11 local with no orphans. Active risk-profile rules blocked 130 candidates. The signal-quality policy remained observe-only (`enforce=false`, sizing disabled), and many accepted signals had unseen calibration buckets; that should remain paper-only until enough outcomes exist.

The major defect was computational. Each ticker's 30-minute bar invoked a separate model scan, creating 7,481 scans rather than one cross-sectional scan per boundary. Queue depth reached 6,267, bar lag reached 600 seconds, and 56 stale bars were dropped. This also defeated the ranker's intended global top-five behavior because each one-symbol scan could return its own “top” candidate.

The fix batches a boundary wave, performs one vectorized cross-sectional scan off the bar-consumer thread, and applies the ranker's global top-five selection. This is a material behavior correction and should receive a full paper-session soak before any live promotion.
