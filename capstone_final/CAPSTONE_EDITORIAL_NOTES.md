Across these samples, the median sentence length was about 20 words; adjacent sentences differed by at least ten words approximately 40% of the time. Longer formal pieces used semicolons to connect closely related independent clauses, direct transitions such as “however” and “while,” practical examples, and qualified claims. The revised report mirrors those higher-level traits: it varies sentence rhythm, uses semicolons deliberately, avoids nearby word repetition where a precise synonym is available, and frames redesign as progress. Research papers listed in `InvestingResearchProject.docx` informed technical organization and terminology, but the author's older documents remained the voice baseline.
